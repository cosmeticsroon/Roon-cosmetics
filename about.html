Who We Are

The ROON Story

ROON Cosmetics was born from a simple but powerful idea: every woman deserves access to premium beauty products that actually work for her skin, her tone, and her life.

Founded in Nairobi in 2024, we set out to curate and deliver the finest skincare, makeup, fragrance and haircare products — at prices that don't require choosing between beauty and bills.

We believe beauty should feel effortless, inclusive and joyful. From the deepest ebony to the fairest porcelain, ROON has something for every woman, every shade, every story.
